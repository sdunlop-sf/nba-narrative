02The composable shelf

Everything the harness can choose from, built once and reused everywhere

The shelf is a living inventory, not a content library. Each item is tagged (channel, eligibility, dates, regulatory flags) so the harness knows what it may use and when. Tap a level to see how it maps to the platform.

When the shelf has a gap

Shelf-gap detection

When nothing on the shelf fits a moment, an optional step drafts a new treatment, checks it against every rule, and holds it for a person to approve. Approved drafts can join the shelf.

Honest about today

Selection, not free assembly

The harness picks the action and offer, then the best approved content for it. It doesn't yet assemble new combinations from hundreds of parts, and learning happens at the action level, not per content variant.

03Inside one moment

Four micro-decisions, each with one job

A decision isn't one big model call. It's a chain of small, specialised decisions, each recorded, with the governed arbitration step in the middle.

1Route

What is this moment?

Koa Decisions

Turns a call note, web visit or event into a typed, confidence-scored signal, such as "price concern 0.8".

Typed signals, not keywords

05Tiered reasoning

Deterministic when it can be. Reasoning when it should be.

Most decisions have a clear winner and are settled by the deterministic tier. Harder calls escalate to more reasoning. Every tier runs inside the same rules and writes the same decision record.

Tier 1

Deterministic arbitration

Weighted scores, a clear winner, no AI model in the path. Explainable by design.

Runs: every decision
Tier 1.5

AI re-rank

When the top two are close, an AI model re-ranks the shortlist using the full context. If it fails, the deterministic winner stands.

Runs: when the gap is narrow (optional add-on)
Tier 2

Human review

The decision waits in a review queue (or Slack) with its rationale. A person picks from the eligible actions or dismisses.

Runs: still ambiguous, or flagged for review
Compose

New treatment

When nothing in the catalogue fits, a draft treatment is composed, checked against every rule, and must be approved before use.

Runs: a gap in the catalogue (optional add-on)

06Resolution depth

Same decision. The right level of detail for each endpoint.

Marketing

Intent

The action, content and timing window. The journey runs the steps.

Service

Intent + treatment

The action plus a script, the eligible options and the reason.

Web & app

Intent + content

The action and approved content to render, or a ranked set for several slots.

Agents

A structured offer

The full offer: code, value, validity and limits, so another agent can negotiate within guardrails.

07Closing the loop

"Accepted" is a proxy. The loop learns from what actually happened.

Immediate

Response

Accepted, declined, ignored, delivered or expired, captured from every channel.

Near-term

Verified outcome

Did they actually renew, buy or stay? Matched to real CRM or Data 360 events within an attribution window.

Measured

Incremental lift

Holdout groups show what the decision added. Learned weights, by action and by segment, follow the evidence.
